Friends of Shirati

Sustaining community and health work.

Our Work Together

Friends of Shirati formed part of the wider network of organizations and supporters connected to health and community work in Shirati. The relationship reflects SHED's ability to bring together formal institutions, community-based supporters and individuals around the shared goal of improving local services.

Key Projects or Contributions

Support networks of this kind were especially important during SHED's earlier years, when rural health and development work often depended on flexible assistance that could respond to practical needs not covered by a major grant. SHED's local presence allowed that support to be directed toward real community priorities and incorporated into ongoing programs.

Why the Partnership Mattered

Small committed supporter groups sustained early SHED work.
