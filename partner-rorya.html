Rorya District Council / Rorya Health Council

Integrating programs with Tanzania's public-health system.

RD

Our Work Together

Rorya's public authorities have worked with SHED on health coordination, facility services, reproductive and child health, HIV-related services, vaccinations and integration with district health priorities — including work through Roche Health Center and maternal and child health outreach.

Key Projects or Contributions

District health coordination; facility and outreach services; maternal/child health and vaccination alignment.

Why the Partnership Mattered

Government alignment is what lets NGO programs become part of the health system rather than parallel to it.
