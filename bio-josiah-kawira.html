Josiah Kawira

Founding Executive Director of SHED Foundation.

Role in SHED’s Establishment

Josiah Kawira was one of SHED’s three founding directors and its first Executive Director, responsible for building the young organization’s structure and early programs.

Education and Professional Background

After studying economics at Bluffton College and Ball State University, he spent more than two decades as Projects Director for Kanisa la Mennonite Tanzania, where his responsibilities included development planning and work with international donor agencies.

Contribution to SHED

He brought experience in administration, finance, development and international partnership to SHED and helped establish the organizational structure that supported its early health and community programs — including the donor-facing discipline that later let SHED administer complex multi-institutional work.

Legacy within SHED

The planning, finance and donor-accountability disciplines that Josiah Kawira installed are the reason SHED could grow from a village initiative into a platform for international research and university partnership. This biography will be expanded as additional archival material is located.
