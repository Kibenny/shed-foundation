Historical Project Partner

Duke University

The 2013 schistosomiasis health-education campaign.

Our Work Together

Duke students worked with SHED and Village Life on a 2013 health-education campaign focused on schistosomiasis and intestinal worms in Roche, Burere and Nyambogo — educating communities about disease, prevention and treatment so parents could make informed decisions about treatment for school-aged children, with DukeEngage/Duke support.

Key Projects or Contributions

Campaign materials and community education sessions across the three villages.

Why the Partnership Mattered

A focused student project with measurable community-health purpose.
