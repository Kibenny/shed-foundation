Support SHED Foundation

If you would like to support SHED Foundation, fund a program, make a donation or explore a partnership, please contact us directly. We welcome conversations with individuals, foundations, institutions and organizations interested in strengthening health, education and sustainable development in northern Tanzania. SHED will provide the appropriate giving information and can discuss how support may be directed toward current programs and priorities.
