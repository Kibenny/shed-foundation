Capt. Josiah Ogada Magatti

Founding General Secretary of SHED Foundation.

Role in SHED’s Establishment

Josiah Magatti was a founding director and SHED’s first General Secretary, keeping the organization’s records, correspondence and administration in order from the start.

Education and Professional Background

His career combined agriculture and economics, military service and extensive administration within schools, church institutions, health programs and Shirati Hospital.

Contribution to SHED

He later became deeply involved in the Tanzania implementation of the EMBLEM research program, helping coordinate personnel, logistics, laboratory development and regional case identification. His career reflected the combination of local institutional knowledge and administrative capacity that became central to SHED’s development.

Legacy within SHED

From General Secretary’s files to multi-site research logistics, Capt. Magatti embodied SHED’s conviction that administration is mission work. This biography will be expanded as additional archival material is located.
