Dr. Esther Lehman Kawira

Founding-Era Medical Director and Medical Leader.

Role in SHED’s Establishment

Although the organizational records distinguish SHED’s formal founding directors from its wider founding leadership, Dr. Esther Kawira is featured prominently because of her central role in building SHED’s medical work from its earliest years.

Medical Leadership

She led Sota Health Clinic, served as SHED’s Medical Director, treated and referred patients with Burkitt lymphoma, and hosted generations of visiting health professionals — physicians, nurses, pharmacists, researchers, students and faculty who learned rural medicine in Shirati.

Research Leadership

As Tanzania Principal Investigator for the NCI-supported EMBLEM study of Burkitt lymphoma, conducted with the National Institutes of Health, Bugando Medical Centre and partners across East Africa, she placed a rural Tanzanian organization at the center of world-class science. See also the EMBLEM Study page.

Legacy within SHED

Her medical leadership helped establish SHED as a credible platform for both rural clinical care and international research — the combination on which SHED’s third decade is now building. This biography will be expanded as additional archival material, including the EMBLEM/NCI leadership photographs, is located.
