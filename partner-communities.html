Our Work Together

SHED's community-led development model gives village governments, water committees, school communities, health committees and residents real roles: identifying priorities, implementing projects and managing local systems — from water-point revenue collection to school nutrition oversight.

Key Projects or Contributions

Village water committees; school and health committees; community meetings that set project priorities; maintenance and oversight structures.

Why the Partnership Mattered

Community ownership is the mechanism by which projects survive after funding ends.
