Rev. Manaen Ngaji Kawira

Founding Chair of SHED Foundation.

Role in SHED’s Establishment

Rev. Manaen Kawira was one of SHED’s three founding directors and its founding chair in 2006. His background connected community leadership, administration and development experience at the moment SHED was being established.

Education and Professional Background

Educated at Hesston College, Bluffton College and Ball State University, he worked in health administration, Tanzanian government service, international development programming and church leadership.

Contribution to SHED

He brought decades of experience in education, health administration, government, development programming and church leadership to the new organization — the combination of credibility and administrative capacity a young Tanzanian NGO needed to be taken seriously by communities, government and international partners alike.

Legacy within SHED

As founding chair, Rev. Kawira set the standard for SHED governance: local leadership accountable to the communities served. This biography will be expanded as additional family, board or archival material is located.
